PaRappa the Rapper @VERSION@

Extract this folder and run PaRappaTheRapper_Recompiled.exe on Windows.
Supply your legally owned USA disc image; no disc or retail BIOS is included.
Keep the CUE and its referenced BIN together. Bundled OpenBIOS is available.

Internal Resolution starts ON at 4x. In Mods, choose saved Native (1x) through
32x; disabling Internal Resolution uses native resolution. Widescreen retains
its existing 21:9 default and Fit to window choice. Generic CD Speed and Fast
Loading packages are excluded. The title uses a digital pad and D-pad directions;
configure your preferred controller in Controls. Authored input mappings remain.

Rhythm Timing Assist remains disabled by default. The audio-buffer target is
60 ms. These are the existing title settings; no new gameplay/audio-quality or
per-title performance improvement is claimed by this release. Current builds use
build-specific disposable snapshots; keep native memory cards when upgrading.
